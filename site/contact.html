Get in touch

We are flexible and have multiple spaces on offer: be sure to reach out to our friendly staff!

Whether you're planning a performance, rehearsal, class, meeting or something else, we'd love to hear about it. You can email us directly or use the form below.

Prefer to send an email?

Write to bookings@goodwoodinstitute.asn.au in your own email app. We’ve prepared a few prompts to help you get started.
