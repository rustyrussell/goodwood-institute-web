Putting a show on the website

In the show's Google Calendar entry, put Publish to website on a line of its own in the notes (the description). It can be any case and can end in a colon. Only lines after that marker are considered website instructions.

Only recognised fields after that marker go on the website. Private booking notes should stay above the marker. The first unrecognised or incomplete non-blank line ends the website instructions: that line and everything after it stays private. Blank lines are allowed. The status page shows what will be published and flags mistakes.

Example

Contact: Jane 0400 123 456 (private, not published)
Publish to website
title: The Importance of Being Earnest
company: Sample Theatre Company
dates: 14–21 Nov
doors open: 7pm
show starts: 7.30pm
dates: Sun 22 Nov
doors open: 1.30pm
show starts: 2pm
show ends: 4.30pm
tickets: https://www.trybooking.com/…
ticket prices: $25, $20 concession
summary: Wilde's comedy of mistaken identity.

The lines

LineWhat it does
Publish to websiteRequired before any public fields. Without it, nothing is published.
DRAFTAfter the publication marker: show on the test website only, never on the main website.
FEATUREDOn its own line. Shows this one under the curtain at the top of the page. Otherwise the next show is used.
title:The show's name. If left out, the calendar entry's title is used.
company:Who is putting the show on.
dates:Which days have performances, if not every day of the calendar entry. For example 14–21 Nov, Sat 21 & Sun 22 Nov, Sundays, Thu–Sat. The doors open: and show starts: lines after it apply to those dates; start a new dates: line for days with different times (like a Sunday matinee). Each date may only be in one dates: line; for two shows on one day, list both times in show starts:.
show starts:Time the show starts, e.g. 7.30pm. Two shows a day: 2pm, 7.30pm. Always write am or pm.
doors open:Time the doors open, e.g. 7pm.
show ends:Time the show finishes, e.g. 10pm. One time, or one for each show time.
tickets:Web address for buying tickets (starting https://).
ticket prices:e.g. $25, $20 concession. (A line starting price: is not published, in case it's the hire price.)
website:The company's or show's web page.
image:Web address of a poster picture (portrait shape works best). Add more image: lines to create a carousel.
summary:One or two sentences about the show, on one line.
suitable for:e.g. All ages or 15+ (coarse language).
duration:e.g. 2 hours including interval.
status:One of: {{ statuses | join(', ') }}. After a dates: line it applies to just those dates.

Capital letters don't matter. Field lines may use a colon, equals sign or a spaced dash, and common forms such as Show starts 7pm work. Happening This Month

Regular hirers (yoga, dance classes…) are set up once on the status page: any calendar entry whose title contains, say, “Kanti Yoga” appears in the timetable for the rest of this month. Their notes don't need anything, but you can add:

HIDEOn its own line. Leaves this one session off the timetable (e.g. a holiday).
status: cancelledShows the session as cancelled.
